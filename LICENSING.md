# Licensing and permissions policy

**Current policy: restricted / all rights reserved.**

Living World is developed publicly, but public visibility is not an open-source licence. Unless a specific file or component contains its own explicit licence or written permission notice, no general permission is granted to reuse, copy, modify, adapt, redistribute, sublicense, publish or commercially exploit that material.

## What this means

- This repository is a public project hub, not a blanket open-source release.
- Selected code and assets may remain private in a separate private repository or storage location.
- Do not assume that code, art, designs, documents, screenshots or other project materials are free to reuse.
- Permission must be obtained in writing from the relevant rights holder before reuse.
- A separate explicit licence may be added to individual components in the future.

## Third-party material

This policy does not claim ownership of, or change the terms applying to, third-party material. Unreal Engine, Cesium, plugins, libraries, datasets, imagery, 3D Tiles, fonts, marketplace assets and other external material remain subject to their own owners' licences and terms.

Do not redistribute third-party material unless its applicable terms expressly allow it. Record source, owner, licence, attribution and redistribution permissions in the project's asset/data register.

Useful official references:
- Unreal Engine licensing: https://www.unrealengine.com/license
- Cesium legal terms: https://cesium.com/legal/terms-of-service/
- Cesium content attribution guidance: https://cesium.com/learn/ion/content-usage-and-attribution-guide/

## Contributions

Contributors retain rights in their own original work unless a separate written agreement provides otherwise. A pull request, issue comment or Discord message does not by itself transfer copyright or grant the public a reuse licence.

Before incorporating a substantial external contribution into a release, the project will agree in writing what permission Living World needs to use, modify, distribute and maintain that contribution. No contributor is promised payment, revenue share, employment or project ownership unless covered by a separate written agreement.

## Public and private repositories

Private code and assets must be stored separately. GitHub does not provide folder-level confidentiality within a public repository. Do not commit restricted material and assume it can be hidden later: copies and Git history may persist even after deletion.

## Requesting permission

For permission requests, contact the project owner through the contact method listed on the Living World GitHub profile. Please identify the exact material and intended use.

This document is a project policy, not legal advice. Obtain appropriate legal advice before accepting significant contributions or distributing a commercial product.
